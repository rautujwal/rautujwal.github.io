Centralized BI Platform / R2P Group / Sydney / May 2026 to Aug 2026

Problem

Release data was fragmented across Jira tickets and Zephyr Scale test cases. The Head of IT, Engineering Leads, and QA teams had no unified view of release readiness, defect verification, or regression trends for their monthly Netspire release. Reporting was a once-daily batch, which was too slow for release decisions.

What I Built

A 3-layer PostgreSQL data mart with incremental, idempotent ETL pipelines orchestrated by Apache Airflow. CDC and strict reconciliation ensure data accuracy. Dual reporting via Streamlit for real-time snapshots and Metabase for historical trends.

Outcome

Replaced daily batch reporting with hourly refresh, a 24x increase in refresh frequency. The Head of IT, Engineering Leads, and QA teams now monitor release readiness and catch delivery risks before deployment. The platform processes 240K+ records monthly across 300+ test cases and 30+ Jira tickets per Netspire release cycle.

PostgreSQL Apache Airflow Python CDC Idempotent ETL Streamlit Metabase REST APIs
Data Engineering & Pipeline Reliability

Architecture: Designed a 3-layer PostgreSQL Data Mart (staging, production, admin/OLAP) with incremental ETL pipelines and strict data lineage to track end-to-end SDLC metrics. Raw data lands in an immutable staging layer before transformation, with a context-ID-driven reverse ETL loop feeding transformed release data back into OLAP.

Reliability: Orchestrated production-ready pipelines using Apache Airflow with parameterized DAGs. Implemented automated logging and monitoring to improve maintainability and simplify debugging. Hourly refresh ensures stakeholders always work with current data.

Data Quality: CDC (Change Data Capture), idempotent merge/upsert operations, and strict reconciliation between layers ensure that every record in the production mart is validated and traceable.

Unified Reporting Solutions

Dual reporting architecture: Streamlit delivers real-time operational snapshots from a context ID-driven transformation flow that collapses data to a 1:1 project grain and loads the final snapshot back to OLAP. Metabase serves historical trend analysis and self-service exploration, allowing users to track current release status and progression over time through the same reporting model.

Feature: Users can monitor current release status and analyse progression over time through a unified reporting model, with the final OLAP snapshot powering operational Streamlit reporting.

Insurance Portfolio Review Prioritisation

Problem

How would you prioritise 1.05M insurance policies when review effort is being spread evenly, regardless of where claims are actually concentrated?

What I Built

A modular, rerunnable SQL workflow using baseline benchmarking, lift scoring, Pareto analysis, tenure cohorts, decile analysis, and stability checks to systematically identify where claim concentration existed.

Outcome

The top 30% of policies generated 55% of total claims, with the top two deciles accounting for 45%. Policy type, location, and tenure showed no meaningful concentration, indicating segment and lifecycle factors were not primary drivers. An interactive dashboard and model performance evaluation assessed potential revenue and customer loyalty risk, creating a repeatable framework for targeted portfolio review.

Operational Price Review Support

Problem

How would you know whether a used vehicle's asking or offer price is fair, and what should you do if the market data suggests it isn't?

What I Built

A model-driven pricing decision support system that estimates an expected market price, benchmarks a proposed price using Z-score analysis to classify pricing risk, and provides plain-language guidance on whether the price is reasonable, overpriced, or underpriced.

Outcome

Users can adjust the proposed price and instantly re-run the assessment, turning the model from a one-time prediction into an iterative pricing decision-support tool. The system surfaces where a price sits relative to the market and what that implies for negotiation or walk-away decisions.

Closed-loop workflow:
Vehicle details → Pricing model → Expected market price → Proposed price → Z-score benchmarking → Risk classification → Plain-language assessment → Recommendation → Price adjustment → Re-evaluation

Tableau / Python / Labour Market

Skills Shortage & Labour Market Analysis

Problem

Are 485 graduate inflows helping ease Australia's labour-market pressure, and does the relationship vary across occupation groups?

What I Built

Analysed IVI job-ad, ABS labour-force, and 485 Graduate Visa data across occupations and states from 2020 to 2025. Used Python to clean and standardise the data, then built an interactive Tableau analytical story comparing graduate inflows with labour-market pressure by occupation.

Outcome

2022 was the peak pressure year, followed by a broad easing in labour-market pressure. Professionals and Managers showed clearer cooling alongside increasing graduate inflows, while other ANZSCO groups showed uneven alignment, indicating no consistent directional relationship between graduate inflows and labour-market pressure across occupations. The analysis highlights where graduate supply appears more aligned with easing pressure and where shortages remain less responsive.
